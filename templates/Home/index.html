FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long does onboarding take?

We set up pilot colleges personally. Timelines depend on your data and drive calendar, and we'll agree them with you.

Is student data secure?

Student privacy is a core design goal: role-based access and consent-first sharing. We'll walk pilot partners through the details.

What will it cost?

Pricing isn't final yet. Pilot colleges use it free, and we plan to keep it free for recruiters and students.

Can we run virtual or hybrid drives?

That's the goal: online tests, video interviews and in-person rounds in one schedule.

Does it integrate with our ERP?

Spreadsheet import/export comes first. ERP and SSO are on the roadmap, shaped by what pilot colleges need.
